D cluster interpretation audit

Question:
Can -log C_d(-y) be interpreted directly as a positive tree/forest EGF?

Result:
Not in the naive Cayley-tree sense.

Reason 1:
u/(1+lambda*y*u) is a geometric series after the w=-y substitution:

u/(1+lambda*y*u) = sum_{r>=0} (-lambda*y)^r u^(r+1).

This is not the Cayley rooted-tree EGF. Cayley rooted trees satisfy T = u*exp(T), with EGF T(u)=sum_{n>=1} n^(n-1) u^n/n!.

Reason 2:
The Sheffer exponent for C_d(-y) contains alternating signs when expanded in lambda*y*u. Therefore exp(...) is a signed weighted assembly in the raw variables, not a manifestly positive forest model.

Reason 3:
The log step does select connected structures, but connected does not automatically mean positive. A sign-reversing cancellation or a renormalized positive species is still needed.

Corrected target:
Find a positive cluster/path model for -log C_d(-y) after the right change of variables or normalization. The likely object is not ordinary Cayley trees but signed/weighted chains or clusters whose cancellations produce the observed positive D-coordinates.

Current exact source:
C_d(w)=w^d P_lambda,d(1/w)
Q_m = (-1)^m s_m = -m [y^m] log C_d(-y)

D-positivity remains supported by exact data, but the naive forest interpretation is not yet a proof.
