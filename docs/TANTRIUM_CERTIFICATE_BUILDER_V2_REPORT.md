# Tantrium Certificate Builder v2 Report

Certificate Builder v2 is used here in the Research OS sense: it packages campaign outcomes into auditable refined-gap certificates and review packets. It does not create final external proof certificates.

## Current Certificate Scope

Recorded scope: `research_os_refined_gap_certificate`

This scope means the campaign reached a stable refined obstruction or work queue. It does not mean the target theorem has been proved.

## Certificate Contents

A v2 research certificate should include:

- campaign identifier and public name
- terminal Research OS status
- candidate count and proof-attempt count
- counterexample-search result and coverage note
- refined subgap or formalization queue
- links to generated campaign files

## Non-Claims

These certificates do not claim:

- Lean or Coq completion
- proof of the synthesized candidate theorems
- global absence of counterexamples
- closure of Goldbach, coefficient frontier, or Gate AB gaps

They are useful because they make the next obstruction precise.
