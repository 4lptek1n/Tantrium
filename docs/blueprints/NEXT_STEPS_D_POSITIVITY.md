# Next steps: D-positivity

## Core theorem to prove

For x=d-2, define Q_m,ell(x) as the lambda^(2 ell) coefficient of (-1)^m s_m.

The target expansion is

Q_m,ell(x) = sum_a D(m,ell,a) binom(x,a)

with

D(m,ell,a) >= 0.

## Why this is central

Vandermonde gives A(m,ell,p,s)=D(m,ell,p+s). Therefore D-positivity implies double-binomial positivity for the Newton moments.

The intended chain is:

D-positivity -> Newton double-binomial positivity -> Hankel/LGV positivity -> C(k,r,s) positivity -> coefficient positivity.

## Known evidence

The analyzer tools/analyze_newton_moment_vandermonde.py verifies the window m<=12, ell<=4 with no negative D rows.

The a=0 edge has a closed positive product formula recorded in results/engine/D_formula_hunt_report.txt.

The ell=0 layer is tied to the signed matching polynomial generated by exp(u - y^2 u^2/4), recorded in results/engine/D_extension_attempt_report.txt.

## Roads already closed

1. Raw Newton recurrence does not give positive transition coefficients.
2. Raw P_lambda,d coefficients are not binomial-positive.
3. Naive Cayley tree interpretation is not correct.
4. A single product formula for all D is not visible.
