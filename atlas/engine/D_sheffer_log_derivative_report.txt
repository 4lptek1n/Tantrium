D Sheffer log-derivative report

Goal: prove D(m,L,a) >= 0 for
Q_m,L(x) = coeff lambda^L in (-1)^m s_m,
Q_m,L(x) = sum_a D(m,L,a) binom(x,a), x=d-2.

1. Reversed polynomial

Let
C_d(w) = w^d P_{lambda,d}(1/w).

Then P'/P at infinity gives
s_m = -m [w^m] log C_d(w).

Therefore
Q_m = (-1)^m s_m = -m [y^m] log C_d(-y).

2. Sheffer coefficient extraction

The Sheffer EGF is
sum_d P_{lambda,d}(z) u^d/d! = exp(z*S(u)+R(u)),
with
S(u)=u/(1-lambda*u),
R(u)=-u^2/(4*(1-lambda*u))-u^2/48*((1-lambda*u)^(-2)-1).

Substitute z=1/w and u -> w*u:

sum_d C_d(w) u^d/d! = exp(S(w*u)/w + R(w*u)).

For w=-y this becomes

C_d(-y) = d! [u^d] exp(
  u/(1+lambda*y*u)
  - y^2*u^2/(4*(1+lambda*y*u))
  - y^2*u^2/48*((1+lambda*y*u)^(-2)-1)
).

Thus the exact Sheffer/log formula is

Q_m,L(x) = coeff lambda^L in -m [y^m] log C_(x+2)(-y).

3. What this proves now

This gives a direct source formula for every D(m,L,a). It bypasses the raw Newton recurrence.

4. What remains

The formula is exact but not yet a complete positivity proof. The exponent contains mixed signs after expansion in lambda*y*u, so positivity is not termwise obvious.

The next proof target is a positive cluster/path interpretation of
-log C_d(-y), or a renormalized coefficient extraction that makes the D-coordinates manifestly nonnegative.

5. Current status

D-positivity is reduced to the Sheffer/log-derivative formula above. The raw Newton-transition induction failed, so the active route is now:
Sheffer reversed polynomial -> log derivative -> positive cluster/path expansion -> D positivity.
