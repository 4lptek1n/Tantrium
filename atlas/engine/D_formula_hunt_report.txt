D formula hunt report

Goal:
Find a manifestly positive closed formula for D(m,ell,a), where
Q_m,ell(x)=sum_a D(m,ell,a) binom(x,a), x=d-2.

Data source:
The table is generated by tools/analyze_newton_moment_vandermonde.py from the exact v1 Newton engine. The static CSV was not present in the repo, so the analyzer is the source of truth.

1. Support laws observed

Let m=2r. Nonzero D rows occur only for parity-compatible even m in the checked window.

For ell<=4 and m<=12:
- 0 <= ell <= r
- degree_x Q_(2r,ell) = r + ell + 1
- active binomial coordinates are a=0..r+ell+1
- all checked D(2r,ell,a) are nonnegative

2. Denominator law

In the checked D table, denominators use only prime 2. This is simpler than the later C(k,r,s) layer, where primes 2 and 3 appear.

Interpretation: D is closer to the primitive heat/Hermite normalization; the factor 3 enters later through tau/log-det recombination.

3. Edge closed form found

The first binomial coordinate a=0 has a clean formula.

For ell=0:
D(2r,0,0)=2^(1-r).

For ell>=1:
D(2r,ell,0)
= 2^(1-r) * 2^(2ell)/(ell!*(2ell-1)!!) * r^2 * product_{i=1}^{ell-1} (r^2-i^2).

This matches the checked data for ell=1..4 and r in the computed range.

Examples:
ell=1: 2^(1-r) * 4*r^2
ell=2: 2^(1-r) * (8/3)*r^2*(r^2-1)
ell=3: 2^(1-r) * (32/45)*r^2*(r^2-1)*(r^2-4)
ell=4: 2^(1-r) * (32/315)*r^2*(r^2-1)*(r^2-4)*(r^2-9)

This is the first genuine closed positive subformula for D.

4. Product-form tests

Simple separated formulas failed. In particular, D does not appear to be of the form
A(r,ell)*B(a) or A(r,ell)*binom(N,a)
across the visible data.

The internal coordinates a>=1 have larger full-rank behavior and do not collapse to a single binomial product.

5. Factorization notes

Sample factorizations show weighted arithmetic, not plain small-count sequences:
D(8,4,9)=518918400 = 2^8 * 3^4 * 5^2 * 7 * 11 * 13.
D(10,2,8)=194731425/2.
D(12,4,11)=9882645003000.

So the full D layer is not a small Catalan/factorial sequence in raw normalization.

6. Current conclusion

A complete closed formula for all D(m,ell,a) has not been found yet.

However, the a=0 edge has a positive product formula, denominators are only powers of 2, and the support law is clean:
deg_x Q_(2r,ell)=r+ell+1.

Next target:
Find a positive formula for interior coordinates a>=1, likely as a finite positive sum extending the a=0 product formula rather than as a single product.
