Operator expansion audit

Goal:
Test whether P_lambda,d(z)=exp(A) z^d gives coefficient positivity directly at the p_k level.

Operator:
A = -1/4 D^2 + lambda*(z D^2 - 1/24 D^3).

Let
P_lambda,d(z)=sum_k p_k(d,lambda) z^(d-k).

Audit:
Each p_k was expanded in x=d-2 and converted to the binomial basis binom(x,a).

Result:
The raw p_k coefficients are not binomial-positive.

First obstructions:
p_2 at lambda^0 has binomial coordinates [-1/2, -1, -1/2].
p_3 at lambda^1 has negative binomial coordinates.
p_4 at lambda^2 has negative binomial coordinates.

Also the signed variants (-1)^k p_k and the Newton-transition signed blocks fail positivity.

Conclusion:
The direct route
P coefficients -> positive binomial p_k -> Newton identities -> D positivity
fails in raw form.

Meaning:
D-positivity is not termwise visible in the polynomial coefficients p_k. It appears only after Newton/logarithmic recombination.

Active route:
Sheffer reversed polynomial -> log derivative -> positive cluster/path expansion -> D positivity.

This strengthens the conclusion that the log-derivative layer, not the raw coefficient layer, is the correct primitive object.
